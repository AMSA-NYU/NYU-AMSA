Membership Access

Join AMSA for free and build attendance toward Tier I. Submit the one-time dues whenever you are ready to qualify for Tier II.

Two ways to join

Start free or submit $25 dues

Joining AMSA does not require payment. Attend three events after joining to reach Tier I. To qualify for Tier II, attend three events and submit the one-time $25 dues. If paying now, include your full name in the payment note so AMSA can match it. Dues are reviewed before Tier II access is approved.
