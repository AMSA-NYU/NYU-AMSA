Glimpse of Clinical Publication

Where medicine meets creative expression.

AMSA’s Glimpse of Clinical Publication (GCP) is a multidisciplinary literary and visual magazine that explores how art and science intersect through the lens of medicine and health. Run through NYU’s largest pre-medical organization, AMSA, GCP highlights powerful stories, innovative research, and creative work that explore what it means to care for others—and ourselves—in today’s healthcare landscape.

Your voice belongs here

Stories, science, and lived experience

We welcome writers, artists, researchers, and thinkers who want to share their lived experiences, scientific insights, and creative expressions. From medical journalism and research commentary to poetry, personal narratives, visual art, and experimental prose, GCP is a space for voices that challenge, inspire, and illuminate.

Selected work will be published across social media platforms and our newsletter, giving contributors meaningful visibility while amplifying causes and conversations that matter in medicine and public health.
