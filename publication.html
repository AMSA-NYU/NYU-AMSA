Student publication Glimpse of Clinical Publication Stories, science, and creative expression through the lens of healthcare. Learn more +

Where medicine meets creative expression.

AMSA’s Glimpse of Clinical Publication (GCP) is a multidisciplinary literary and visual magazine that explores how art and science intersect through medicine and health. Run through NYU’s AMSA chapter, GCP highlights stories, research, and creative work about caring for others—and ourselves.

Your voice belongs here

We welcome writers, artists, researchers, and thinkers sharing lived experiences, scientific insights, and creative work that challenges, inspires, and illuminates.

What we publish

  • Medical journalism and research commentary
  • Poetry and personal narratives
  • Visual art and experimental prose

Selected work is shared across social media and our newsletter, giving contributors visibility and amplifying conversations in medicine and public health.

If you’re interested in storytelling, science, and social impact, bring your perspective to GCP.
